Reading order
-------------

The five sections should be read in filename order.  Section 1 states the
theorem and separates internal work from imported results.  Section 2 performs
the phase and orientation-chart calculations.  Section 3 introduces exactly
the motivic vocabulary used later.  Section 4 derives universal framed
rigidity from E2--E4.  Section 5 specializes and returns to finite scissors
congruence.

Status
------

The manuscript is an unattributed research draft.  It proves an implication
from E1--E8 and explicitly leaves E2--E5 as the relative-motivic bridge.  The
PDF and TeX source are published together so every claim can be inspected and
rebuilt.  Publication here does not assert an unconditional proof, journal
acceptance, independent validation, priority, or a repository-wide license.
