Specialization to scissors congruence
=====================================

Specialization
--------------

The physical tetrahedron gives a complex point of one rational parameter
chart.  E4 says specialization commutes with the reduced coproduct, so the
universal zero-coproduct class supplies an actual membership proof in the
kernel of the field-level coproduct.  E5 says that pullback of the universal
coefficient has exactly the
endpoint class :math:`c_G` of the associated quadric configuration, with the
required frame, sign, projective-duality, and Tate-twist conventions.  This is
an equality of classes, not merely of regulators, and it is an external bridge
rather than a consequence of ordinary cohomological pullback.

The weight-two comparison
-------------------------

E6 records the field-level injections extracted from Goncharov's proved
weight-two results, and E7 compares the spherical presentations.  The
manuscript states the normalization, face order, orientation, and Tate twist
which E5 must preserve.  It does not assume the conjectural general chain map
discussed elsewhere in Goncharov's paper.

Removing the two weakenings
---------------------------

The comparison first gives equality in the reduced rational spherical target.
E8 records the exact sequence from full to reduced scissors classes, injective
rationalization, the suspension-volume identity, area injectivity, and the
cancellation theorem producing a finite list of paired isometric pieces.  E1
supplies equality of Regge volumes.  Lean checks that these statements force
the full additive defect to vanish and then imply a finite geodesic
equidecomposition.
