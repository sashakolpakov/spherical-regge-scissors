Specialization to scissors congruence
=====================================

Specialization
--------------

The physical tetrahedron gives a complex point of one rational parameter
chart.  Pullback of the universal relative motive and its frame gives the
quadric configuration belonging to that tetrahedron.  Because the universal
Regge defect is zero, its specialized framed defect is also zero.

The weight-two comparison
-------------------------

Goncharov's proved weight-two results identify primitive quadric coefficients
with the relevant weight-two scissors complex and give the spherical map.
The manuscript checks that its relative-motive normalization, face order,
orientation, and Tate twist agree with this comparison.  It does not assume
the conjectural general chain map discussed elsewhere in Goncharov's paper.

Removing the two weakenings
---------------------------

The comparison first gives equality after rationalization and spherical
suspension.  Two standard facts finish the argument:

1. the required spherical scissors group is a rational vector space in the
   relevant range, so rational equality is integral equality; and
2. the volume of a spherical suspension is :math:`\frac{\pi}{2}` times the
   area of its base.  The Regge mates have equal volume, so the possible
   suspension-kernel term vanishes.

Equality in the spherical scissors group is then equivalent to a finite
geodesic equidecomposition, which is the asserted scissors congruence.
